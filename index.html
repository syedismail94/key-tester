How Key Tester of keyboard, mouse and touchpad web tool Works

Key Tester of keyboard, mouse and touchpad this web tool runs 100% inside your browser. It highlights keys, mouse buttons, and touchpad zones in real time so you can quickly see what is working and what is not.

Follow these simple steps:

  • 1️⃣ Open this page on your laptop or PC.
  • 2️⃣ Click once anywhere on the page to make sure the browser is active.
  • 3️⃣ Press any key on your Windows or Mac keyboard – the same key will light up on the on-screen keyboard.
  • 4️⃣ Move to the Mouse Tester section and click left, right, middle, and scroll – counters will increase live.
  • 5️⃣ Use the Touchpad Tester on laptops – one-finger tap, two-finger tap, and supported middle tap will highlight the zones.
  • 6️⃣ If a key or button does not highlight here, it may be a hardware, OS, or browser limitation.

You may freely use this tool to test your own keyboard, mouse, and touchpad. Do not use this tool to monitor, track, or record the activity of other people’s devices.
