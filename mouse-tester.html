How Mouse Tester with Tap & Scroll Counter Works

Mouse Tester with tap and scroll counter is a simple web-based tool that runs 100% inside your browser. It allows you to test your mouse buttons and scroll wheel in real time by counting every click and movement instantly.

Follow these simple steps:

  • 1️⃣ Open this page on your laptop or desktop computer.
  • 2️⃣ Click once anywhere on the page to activate the mouse tester.
  • 3️⃣ Click the Left Mouse Button – the left click counter will increase immediately.
  • 4️⃣ Click the Right Mouse Button – the right click counter will update live.
  • 5️⃣ Press the Middle Mouse Button (scroll wheel click) to test middle click support.
  • 6️⃣ Scroll the mouse wheel up or down – scroll activity will be detected and counted.
  • 7️⃣ If any button or scroll action does not register, it may indicate a mouse hardware or driver issue.

This mouse tester is intended for personal hardware testing only. Do not use this tool to track or monitor mouse activity on other people’s devices.
