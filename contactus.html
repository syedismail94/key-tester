Before you contact

keytester.online runs fully in your browser. Some keys (like Fn, hardware sound keys, or special laptop shortcuts) are sometimes handled directly by the operating system and never sent to the website. In those cases, no online tester can detect them.

If you still feel something is wrong or confusing, send me a message with as much detail as possible. I’ll use your feedback to improve the tool and make it more reliable and user-friendly over time.
